# `PAT-DSN-007` — Cover the Title: the Emphasis Alone Must Point at the Evidence

## 1. Statement
A slide carries two readings that must agree. With only the takeaway titles, the deck tells its story (`PRIN-STR-004`). With the title, subtitle, cards and notes covered, leaving the chart with its axes and direct labels, the strongest emphasis on the slide (focal colour, weight, size) still lands on the marks that prove the title. If the eye goes elsewhere, the design contradicts the words.

## 2. Why It Matters
Audiences look at the chart before or while they read the title. When the focal colour sits on a different bar, or the heaviest line is a context series, the slide asks the viewer to believe the title against what the picture stresses. Checking the picture alone catches emphasis that drifted during the build, which a text-led review misses because the title tells the reviewer where to look.

## 3. Agent Application
- **G4 (`deck-reviewer`):** for each data slide, look at the render as if the text blocks were hidden. Name the first mark the eye lands on, then compare it with the marks the title is about (`G4.6`, `G4.8`).
- **Fix the emphasis, not the words:** move the focal colour, demote a competing series to grey, or change the weight or size; change the title only if the data favour the other mark (`PAT-VIS-014`).
- **Image and quote slides:** the focal panel or focal quote is the one the title is about (`D-107`, `D-108`).
- **No added text:** passing the test never means adding a label that says where to look.

## 4. Testable Checks
- **Focal match:** the mark with the strongest emphasis belongs to the series, category or panel the title names.
- **Single winner:** no second mark has equal or stronger emphasis (a second accent, a dark context line, a larger panel).
- **Picture-only read recorded:** the G4 report states, per data slide, what the chart stresses without its text.

## 5. Good vs. Bad Example
- **Bad:** the title says Harbor Street is furthest behind, but the heatmap's darkest cell is another store's wait time, so with the title covered the eye goes to the wrong row.
- **Good:** the Harbor Street row is the only focal-coloured row; with the title covered, a viewer still picks it out first.
