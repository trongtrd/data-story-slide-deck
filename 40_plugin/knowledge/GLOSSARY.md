# Glossary (`20_knowledge/GLOSSARY.md`)

Canonical vocabulary for **Data Story Slide Deck**. Agents and contributors must use these exact terms and avoid the listed synonyms. When a new term is settled during a session, update this file **inline immediately** (D-007, D-013). Definitions must remain 1–2 tight sentences with zero implementation prose.

---

## Part 1: Storytelling Terms (Shipped with the Plugin)

**Audience**:
The specific decision-makers or stakeholders who will receive the communication, defined by their priorities, biases, and decision authority—not a generic demographic group.
_Avoid_: Readers, viewers, users, general public

**Primary Action**:
The single concrete decision, approval, or next step the presenter needs the Audience to take as a result of the presentation.
_Avoid_: Goal, objective, multiple asks

**Stakes**:
The concrete benefits to the Audience if they take the Primary Action and the concrete risks if they do not.
_Avoid_: Impact, value proposition

**Big Idea**:
A single complete sentence that states the presenter's specific point of view and conveys the Stakes for the Audience.
_Avoid_: Theme, topic sentence, thesis, summary

**Exploratory Analysis**:
The analyst-facing process of investigating raw data to uncover patterns and candidate insights before deciding what to communicate.
_Avoid_: Data storytelling, explanatory work

**Explanatory Communication**:
The audience-facing discipline of translating selected findings into a focused visual narrative that drives understanding or action.
_Avoid_: Data dump, exploratory chart, reporting

**Narrative Tension**:
The gap that matters to the Audience between the current reality (or risk) and the desired outcome, which creates the need for resolution.
_Avoid_: Analyst struggle, methodology complexity, drama

**Narrative Arc**:
A five-part story structure—Plot, Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, and Resolution—that organizes presentation content around Narrative Tension rather than project chronology.
_Avoid_: Chronological flow, linear agenda, project timeline

**Story Mountain**:
A simplified three-part story structure consisting of Plot (context), Twist (complicating tension), and Ending (recommended action).
_Avoid_: Three-act play

**Takeaway Title**:
A concise, point-of-view sentence placed at the top of a slide that answers "So what?" and tells the Audience what conclusion to draw from the slide.
_Avoid_: Topic title, label title, descriptive header

**Descriptive Chart Title**:
A short, neutral label placed directly above a graph that states what metric, dimension, and time period are being plotted.
_Avoid_: Takeaway title, slide headline

**Horizontal Logic**:
The property where reading only the Takeaway Titles across all slides in sequence tells the complete, coherent story of the presentation.
_Avoid_: Slide transitions, deck outline

**Vertical Logic**:
The property where every element on a single slide—Takeaway Title, Descriptive Chart Title, visual, annotations, and supporting text—reinforces the same single point.
_Avoid_: Slide consistency, grid alignment

**One-Sided Layout**:
A slide structure that gives a single primary visual a large prominent area, paired with a Takeaway Title across the top and concise supporting text on one side.
_Avoid_: Single-column slide

**Two-Sided Layout**:
A slide structure divided into left and right vertical zones to compare two scenarios side by side or pair two complementary views of the same point.
_Avoid_: Two-column text slide, split screen

**Delivery Mode**:
The consumption context of a deck—either **Live Presentation** (speaker is present to narrate and reveal piecewise) or **Standalone Deck** (consumed asynchronously as a leave-behind or pre-read requiring self-sufficient text).
_Avoid_: Channel, medium

**Visual Clutter**:
Any visual element on a graph or slide—such as borders, unnecessary gridlines, tick marks, redundant labels, or decorative effects—that consumes cognitive load without adding informative value.
_Avoid_: Minimalism, blank space

**Greyscale Baseline**:
The practice of formatting all chart elements in neutral shades of grey first so that sparing accent color can be applied deliberately to direct attention.
_Avoid_: Monochrome theme, black-and-white chart

**Small Multiples**:
A grid or panel of compact charts sharing the same visual structure and axes, each displaying a different slice of the data (such as a region or role type) to prevent overloading a single chart.
_Avoid_: Sub-charts, mini-graphs, dashboard tiles

**Progressive Reveal**:
The live delivery technique of building a complex visual or multi-chart comparison across sequential slide states—adding one series, panel, or takeaway at a time so the Audience absorbs each point before the next appears.
_Avoid_: Slide transitions, decorative animations, bullet fly-ins

**Color Tie**:
The technique of formatting key words in a Takeaway Title or text annotation in the exact same accent color used to highlight the corresponding data series in the graph.
_Avoid_: Color-coded text, decorative colored fonts

**Reading Note**:
One short sentence that states a chart's unit, base or coverage, set in source-line styling directly above the source; it never takes card or highlight styling. How to read the chart goes in the speaker notes, never on the slide (`D-058`, `D-059`). An essential caveat also goes here, never on a card.
_Avoid_: Caveat box, disclaimer card, warning callout, "how to read" note

**Evidence Ledger**:
The list in the storyline of each research question's key variables with their direction, statistical significance and source cell, null results included; titles and the Big Idea may not contradict it (`D-040`).
_Avoid_: Results summary, findings table, key stats

**Shared-Understanding Objective**:
A deck whose audience is asked to understand findings rather than make a decision; it has no Primary Action and closes on what the findings mean (`D-040`).
_Avoid_: No-ask deck, information-only deck

**Visual Job**:
The comparison a takeaway title asks the chart to prove (trend, change between two points, rank, share, deviation, relationship, look-up, and so on); it selects the row of the chart-selection matrix (`PAT-VIS-003`, `D-053`).
_Avoid_: Chart purpose, data type, chart category

**Familiarity Tier**:
How much decoding a chart form asks of a general audience: Tier 1 default workhorses, Tier 2 conditional forms with an entry rule, Tier 3 specialist forms behind the override (`PRIN-VIS-007`, `D-053`).
_Avoid_: Chart level, advanced chart, fancy chart

**Data Table Slide**:
A slide whose body is a native, editable table for looking up exact values in mixed units; every number traces to `inputs/`, and one row, column or up to three cells carry the accent (`PAT-VIS-006`, `D-055`).
_Avoid_: Data dump, spreadsheet slide, chart table

**Key Slide**:
A non-chart slide drawn on the dark key background so it stands apart from ordinary slides: every Section Divider, the closing decision slide by default, and at most two others with a stated reason. Cards keep their light colours, and the accent takes the kit's lighter tint (`D-063`).
_Avoid_: Dark mode, hero slide, highlight slide

**Section Divider**:
A Key Slide that opens a section: a large section number, the section's claim as its title, and one line naming the question the section answers; no chart, cards or notes on the slide (`D-063`).
_Avoid_: Agenda slide, title card, transition slide

**Executive Summary**:
The first main-deck slide of a pre-read whose audience needs the answer first: the answer as the title, then two to four numbered points, one per section, each with a bold lead sentence and the slides that prove it (`PAT-STR-004`, `D-075`).
_Avoid_: Agenda, overview slide, key takeaways slide

**Evidence Columns**:
A decision slide in which one recommendation line rests on two or three equal columns; each column has a criterion heading of at most three words, a one-sentence finding and one small chart (`PAT-DSN-001`, `D-075`).
_Avoid_: Pros and cons slide, scorecard slide, three-up chart row

**Big-Number Tile**:
One traced number shown large with a short caption on a statement slide; one to three per slide, at most one in the focal colour; a single tile sits beside the main card (`D-064`, `SRC-SWD-012`).
_Avoid_: KPI card, stat box, BAN

**Spotlight Slide**:
The one big-number slide per deck allowed to fill the slide with the kit's focal colour and white text, SWD's simple-text exception to sparing colour (`D-064`).
_Avoid_: Accent slide, colour slide, hero slide

**Waterfall**:
A bar chart that bridges a start value and an end value through signed changes floating at the running total, with grey totals, focal-coloured losses, tinted gains and thin connector lines; in the `changes_only` form the changes grow from a zero-length start and a derived net bar closes them (`D-067`).
_Avoid_: Bridge chart, cascade, truncated waterfall

**Diagram spec**:
The content of a native-shape diagram slide (`layout_type: diagram`): its type (`process_flow`, later timeline and 2×2), the items with labels and sources, and which one or two are focal. The renderer computes all geometry; connectors are glued right-angle lines (`D-069`).
_Avoid_: Hand-placed diagram, SmartArt, picture of a diagram

**Quadrant matrix**:
A two-factor grid read by the corner an item sits in: either dots placed by measured or judged values (positioning), or four named outcomes with one chosen (scenario). Items never sit on an axis, and cells are neutral except the focal one (`D-069`).
_Avoid_: Four-colour quadrant chart, BCG-style bubble grid

**Milestone timeline**:
A dated axis on which events sit at their real dates, with labels staggered above and below the line and one or two focal milestones; the bar form is a Gantt chart for durations and overlap. Dates are never spaced evenly (`D-069`, `ANTI-VIS-004`).
_Avoid_: Equally spaced timeline, roadmap graphic

**Decision flowchart**:
A diagram of branching logic: shape carries the node type (terminator for start and end, rectangle for a step, diamond for a short question), the main path runs left to right, each decision's other exit drops to a row below, and every exit is labelled with its answer beside the line where it leaves the decision (`D-071`).
_Avoid_: Decision tree (for a flowchart), answers written in the child box

**Swimlane**:
A diagram with one horizontal lane per owner and the steps in process order, so every hand-off between owners is a visible arrow; a hand-off keeps its column when the lanes between are free, and the riskiest hand-off takes the accent (`D-071`).
_Avoid_: Cross-functional chart, process map (for this form)

**Tree bus**:
The connector form of a tree diagram: a parent's children hang from one shared trunk joined at right angles, drawn as glued elbow connectors that share the trunk; the root sits on the left for issue and driver trees and on top for org charts (`D-071`).
_Avoid_: Spider connectors, mind map

**Nominal categories**:
Categories with no order of their own (brands, regions, reasons, topics); a bar or dot-plot chart sorts them by value, largest first (`PAT-VIS-004`, `D-073`).
_Avoid_: Unordered labels, plain categories

**Ordinal categories**:
Categories with an order of their own (time periods, age bands, rating bands, Likert and other answer scales); a chart keeps that order and never sorts them by size (`PAT-VIS-004`, `D-073`).
_Avoid_: Ranked categories, ordered labels

**Heatmap table**:
A table whose number cells keep their values and gain a fill in one hue of the kit's comparison colour, stepped from light to full, so the darkest cells carry the title's message; scales run per column (mixed units) or shared (one unit, with a scale key) (`D-068`).
_Avoid_: Heat map chart, conditional formatting, red-green traffic light

**Scatter Section**:
A named region of a scatter plot, bounded by a dashed threshold line, that gives the audience a ready label for a group of points ("waits over 5 min"); its name is in the accent when most of its points are focal (`D-065`).
_Avoid_: Quadrant box, zone, cluster

**Reading Aid**:
What teaches a less familiar chart form to its audience: build steps in the speaker notes for live delivery, or a how-to-read note or annotations for standalone and hybrid decks (`PRIN-VIS-007`).
_Avoid_: Legend, chart explanation, tutorial

**Strip Plot**:
One dot per item along a single value axis, with rows for different measures sharing that axis; dots are packed so none hides another, context items are grey and the one item the title names is in the accent with its value and rank beside the row (`PAT-VIS-013`, `D-086`). Used to show where one item sits among many.
_Avoid_: Jitter plot, beeswarm (as the name of the form), 1D scatter

**Side Column**:
A column of signed text at the right edge of a horizontal bar chart, one value per bar, for each bar's change or gap against a reference such as last year or plan; the bars keep the level (`PAT-VIS-004`, `D-095`).
_Avoid_: Delta column, variance column, data table

## Part 2: System Terms (Pipeline & Architecture)

**Frontier Question**:
A decision question in a grilling round whose prerequisites are already settled and whose answer cannot be looked up from the provided input files.
_Avoid_: Prompt, questionnaire item

**Checkpoint Gate**:
One of the four mandatory or lightweight human alignment stops in the deck creation pipeline (`G1 Context Brief`, `G2 Storyline`, `G3 Visual Plan`, `G4 Draft Review`) that writes a stateful artifact to disk.
_Avoid_: Approval step, pause

**Slide Spec (`slide_spec.yaml`)**:
The format-agnostic intermediate artifact produced after Checkpoint G3 that defines each slide's Takeaway Title, layout, visual/chart specification, annotations, and emphasis rules for downstream renderers.
_Avoid_: Draft deck, outline file

**Knowledge Card**:
An atomic markdown file in `20_knowledge/` (`PRIN-*`, `PAT-*`, `ANTI-*`, `CHK-*`, `EX-*`, `LANG-*`) containing a synthesized rule, agent instructions, and testable checks.
_Avoid_: Note, article, wiki page

**Renderer**:
A downstream build module in `40_plugin/scripts/` that compiles a `Slide Spec` into a final target format (native editable `.pptx` or interactive `.html`).
_Avoid_: Exporter, converter

**Approval Stamp**:
The `approvals.<gate>` record (approver, UTC time, content hash) written into a gate artifact only after the user explicitly approves it; a later edit breaks the hash and reopens the gate (D-021).
_Avoid_: Sign-off flag, `status: approved` alone

**QA Render**:
A slide image produced from the compiled `.pptx` itself (PowerPoint, else LibreOffice → PDF → PNG) with a manifest of the renderer and source hashes, and the only image accepted as Checkpoint G4 visual evidence for PowerPoint decks (D-020, D-044).
_Avoid_: Preview PNG, screenshot, Pillow render

**Self-Attested Flag**:
A boolean an agent sets about its own work (such as `*_verified: true` or a `pass` gate audit) that no script can confirm; reported for transparency but never counted as evidence.
_Avoid_: Verified check, automated pass

**Design System**:
The written set of look decisions every deck follows: one document (`contracts/design_system.md`) and one token file (`contracts/design_tokens.yaml`) that fix faces, type roles and sizes, text colours, spacing and how components are drawn. Named in `deck_meta.design_system`; `editorial` for new decks, `classic` when the field is absent (`PRIN-DSN-010`, `D-081`, `D-092`).
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, template (the generated PowerPoint template is one output of the system, not the system)

**Design Layer**:
Everything about how a deck looks that the Design System owns: typeface, type sizes, text colours, spacing, card and rule drawing, chart chrome, title and divider treatment. It never changes the SWD layer (words, numbers, focal and context roles, Color Tie spans, layout and chart type), which the content digest guards (`D-078`).
_Avoid_: Styling pass, cosmetics, look and feel

**Finish Pass**:
The `deck-finisher` step after the agent review passes G4 and before the owner's human G4: it scores the rendered slides, then changes only the fields of a slide's `finish` block (title break, diagram or chart height, side-card columns) and proves with the content digest that the content is unchanged (`D-091`).
_Avoid_: Polish pass, beautify step, final touch-up
