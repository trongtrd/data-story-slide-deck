# Northvale Health — Nursing Workforce Review (fictional benchmark data)

Since January 2024 all 1,800 nurses rotate to a different unit each month.

- Outpatient pilot clinics that kept fixed teams saw 18.6 patients per nurse shift, versus 15.1 under monthly rotation.
- Competency scores (1–5) for new-graduate nurses fell from 3.9 to 3.2 in Critical Care, 3.8 to 3.3 in Emergency, and 4.0 to 3.8 in Outpatient. Experienced and senior nurses held steady.
- First-year turnover rose from 14% to 27% in Critical Care, 15% to 24% in Emergency, and 11% to 16% in Outpatient.
- Modeled annual savings of unit-based staffing: $2.6M agency spend and $1.7M turnover replacement.
- Open weekend shifts per month would move from 6 to 5 (Critical Care), 9 to 8 (Emergency), and stay at 2 (Outpatient).
