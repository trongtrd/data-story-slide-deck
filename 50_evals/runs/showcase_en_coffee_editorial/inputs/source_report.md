# Copperleaf Coffee — Menu and Staffing Review (fictional benchmark data)

Year-over-year sales change by drink (FY2025 vs. FY2024): Espresso Tonic +15%, Iced Matcha +12%, Classic Americano +9%, Flat White +7%, Chai Latte +4%, Honey Lavender Latte +2%, Maple Oat Latte -3%, Cocoa Crunch Frappé -6%, Hazelnut Cloud Mocha -14%, Mint Chill Cold Brew -18%.
Store staffing fell from 738 in January to 718 in July against a plan rising to 760; the forecast ends December at 730.
Year-end plan vs. forecast by role: baristas 420 vs. 398, shift supervisors 140 vs. 134, store managers 140 vs. 138, bakers 60 vs. 60.
Modeled FY2026 sales recovery: $1.5M from filling barista roles and $0.9M from refreshing the two weakest seasonal drinks.
Operations rated the effort of six recovery initiatives on a 1 to 6 scale (filling barista roles 2, refreshing seasonal drinks 1, extending evening hours 2, a loyalty app 4, remodelling flagships 5, price changes 4); the modeled sales recovery of each is in the initiatives file.

## Recruiting plan for the 22 open barista roles

The budget is approved on 15 January and the roles open on 1 February. The first hiring wave starts on 15 March and is trained by 31 March; the second wave starts on 30 April. All 22 roles are filled by 30 June, and a 90-day review follows on 15 September. Each hire trains on shift for two weeks.

## Applicant screening

Every barista applicant takes a paid four-hour trial shift. Applicants without barista experience first join a two-week training cohort and then take the trial shift. Applicants who pass the trial receive an offer; the others are invited to reapply.

## Hiring hand-offs

The store manager requests the hire; HR posts the role and screens the applicants; a regional trainer runs the trial shift; the store manager decides; HR sends the offer. Applicants wait a median of five days for a regional trainer, the longest wait between any two steps.
