# Source Registry (`10_sources/SOURCES.md`)

Master registry of all external sources studied for **Data Story Slide Deck**.
- **Primary Tier (`primary`):** *storytelling with data* (SWD) materials — the foundational authority for this project (D-003).
- **Secondary Tier (`secondary`):** Complementary frameworks used for cross-checking and filling gaps (subject to `20_knowledge/CONFLICTS.md` and batch user approval per D-003, D-004).
- **Copyright Rule:** Raw files in `10_sources/raw/` are gitignored for local study only. All notes in `10_sources/notes/` must be paraphrased in our own words.
